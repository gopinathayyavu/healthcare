Good to know

Before your visit

Do I need to book in advance?

Advance booking is recommended so you can choose your preferred time. Walk-ins are subject to availability.

Can I choose a therapist?

Therapist preferences can be requested during booking and are confirmed based on availability.

Are these medical treatments?

HealthCar offers wellness experiences and does not replace diagnosis or treatment from a qualified medical professional.
